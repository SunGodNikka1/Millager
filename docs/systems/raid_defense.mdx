# Raid Defense

When a village is raided, Millager reinforcements automatically deploy in **alternating waves** of cavalry and infantry. A green boss bar tracks the reinforcement progress.

## How It Works

Once at least one raid group has spawned, the defense system initializes:

1. A **timer** counts down based on difficulty and village size
2. At zero, a wave of Millagers spawns near the village center
3. Waves alternate: **cavalry** (Lancer/Scouter) on odd waves, **infantry** (Archer/Swordmaster/Mauler/Rioter/Doctor) on even waves
4. Up to **11 waves** total; after the 11th, defense is "fully deployed"

## Timer Formula

```
maxTimer = clamp(2100 - beds × 175 + lastWaveHp, 1400, 6300)
```

- **More beds** in the village → faster reinforcements (more villagers to protect)
- **Higher total HP** from the last wave → longer wait for the next wave

### Actual Wait Times

| Difficulty | Decrement | Wait Range |
|------------|-----------|------------|
| Easy | 4/tick | 17s – 1m 19s |
| Normal | 2/tick | 35s – 2m 37s |
| Hard | 1/tick | 1m 10s – 5m 15s |

## Spawn Location

Millagers prefer to spawn near the village **MEETING point** (bell square), then the raid's bounding box, and finally random positions within 32 blocks of the village center as a fallback.

## Boss Bar

The defense system displays a **green boss bar** alongside the vanilla red raid bar:

- **"Reinforcements"** — timer counting down, progress filling up
- **"Cavalry Deployed"** / **"Infantry Deployed"** — 5-second display after each wave
- **"Forces Fully Deployed"** — permanent full bar after all 11 waves

The boss bar only appears for players within the raid's active radius (same as the vanilla raid bar).

## Persistence

Defense state is saved per village (keyed by `center.asLong()`) in `millager_raids` data. State cleans up when the raid ends (victory, defeat, or expiration).

## Grand Battle

When a raid is **won** with all 11 waves deployed, all players in the raid radius earn the hidden **"War of the Century"** challenge advancement.

## Game Rule

Disable reinforcements: `/gamerule millager:raid_defenses false`
