# Elixir & NITWIT Curing

[Elixir](/content/items/elixir) has two powerful functions: curing harmful status effects on the player, and **curing NITWIT villagers** into normal villagers.

## Curing NITWIT Villagers

NITWIT villagers (green-coated, unemployed villagers that cannot take a profession) can be **cured** with Elixir:

1. Find a NITWIT villager
2. Hold an Elixir in your main hand
3. Right-click the NITWIT

The NITWIT transforms into an **unemployed normal villager**, emitting particles and sounds. The villager drops 25–36 Emerald Blocks as a reward.

<Callout variant="info">
This interaction unlocks the hidden challenge advancement **"A Cure for the Incurable"**.
</Callout>

## How NITWITs Detect Elixir

When a player holding Elixir comes within 8 blocks of a NITWIT:
- The NITWIT displays an Emerald Block in its main hand
- It turns to look at the player
- The held item has a 0% drop chance (cannot be exploited)

## Curing Status Effects

When a player drinks Elixir:
- **All harmful status effects** are removed (Poison, Wither, Blindness, etc.)
- **8 positive effects** are applied for 30 minutes (see the [Elixir](/content/items/elixir) page for the full list)

This makes Elixir an excellent emergency item during tough fights.

## Elixir Recipe

Elixir is brewed in the [Brewing Cauldron](/content/blocks/brewing_cauldron) from [Liquor](/content/items/liquor) and rare ingredients. See the [Brewing](/systems/brewing) guide for the complete pipeline.
