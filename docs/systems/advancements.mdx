# Advancements

Millager adds **9 custom advancements** across two vanilla tabs: **Husbandry** and **Adventure**. Several use custom trigger types unique to the mod.

## Husbandry Tab

```
Root (minecraft:husbandry/root)
├── Just a Sip                      ─ brew a bottle of Liquor
│   └── Local Distillery            ─ brew with all 4 ingredients (goal)
└── Wondrous Elixir                 ─ drink an Elixir (goal)
    └── A Cure for the Incurable    ─ cure a NITWIT with Elixir (hidden, challenge)
```

| Advancement | Frame | Trigger | Description |
|-------------|-------|---------|-------------|
| **Just a Sip** | task | `millager:obtain_liquor` | Brew a bottle of Liquor |
| **Local Distillery** | goal | `millager:brew_liquor` | Brew using wheat, potato, beetroot, and wheat seeds |
| **Wondrous Elixir** | goal | `minecraft:consume_item` | Drink an Elixir |
| **A Cure for the Incurable** | challenge | `millager:heal_nitwit` | Cure a NITWIT villager with Elixir |

## Adventure Tab

```
kill_a_mob (minecraft:adventure/kill_a_mob)
└── Carpet Bombing                  ─ kill 1+ mobs with TNT on a Stick
    └── Tactical Strike             ─ kill 6+ mobs at once with TNT on a Stick (challenge)

trade (minecraft:adventure/trade)
└── Arms Dealer                     ─ trade lethal goods with a Commander (goal)

hero_of_the_village (minecraft:adventure/hero_of_the_village)
└── War of the Century              ─ win a raid with all reinforcements (hidden, challenge)

totem_of_undying (minecraft:adventure/totem_of_undying)
└── Multi-Core Healing              ─ have 2+ Totems of Healing in inventory (goal)
```

| Advancement | Frame | Trigger | Description |
|-------------|-------|---------|-------------|
| **Carpet Bombing** | task | `millager:tnt_kill` | Kill a hostile mob with TNT on a Stick |
| **Tactical Strike** | challenge | `millager:tnt_kill` (min 6) | Kill 6+ hostile mobs at once with one TNT on a Stick |
| **Arms Dealer** | goal | `minecraft:villager_trade` | Trade lethal goods with a Commander |
| **War of the Century** | challenge | `millager:grand_battle` | Win a raid with all 11 reinforcement waves deployed |
| **Multi-Core Healing** | goal | `millager:hold_totems` | Have 2+ Totems of Healing in your inventory |
