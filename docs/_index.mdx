# Millager

**Millager** adds a standing military to the villager side — infantry, cavalry, and constructs — with AI formation patrols, raid reinforcement waves, and garrisoned Command Posts.

## Quick Links

- [Meet the Millagers](@millager:archer_spawn_egg) — seven specialized combat units with unique AI
- [Raid Defense](@millager:totem_of_healing) — reinforcements deploy in waves, green boss bar
- [Wild Patrols]($systems/patrols) — infantry squads and cavalry scouts roam the wilderness
- [Explosive Arsenal](@millager:tnt_on_a_stick) — TNT on a Stick, Molotov Cocktails, Explosive Arrows

## Key Systems

| System | Summary |
|--------|---------|
| [Commander Trading]($systems/commander_trading) | A new villager profession using the Sand Table as job site. 5 trade levels. |
| [Brewing]($systems/brewing) | Liquor Cauldron → Brewing Cauldron → Elixir. Crop-based multi-stage brewing. |
| [Elixir & NITWIT Curing]($systems/elixir_interaction) | Cure NITWIT villagers with Elixir, transforming them into normal villagers. |
| [Raid Defense]($systems/raid_defense) | Millagers automatically reinforce your village during raids. Alternating cavalry/infantry waves. |
| [Patrols]($systems/patrols) | Wild Millager patrols spawn after 3 in-game days. Infantry squads and cavalry scouts. |
| [Command Post]($systems/command_post) | Worldgen structure across forests, plains, and mountains. Loot and explorer maps. |
| [Advancements]($systems/advancements) | 9 custom advancements across Husbandry and Adventure tabs. |
| [Game Rules]($systems/game_rules) | `/gamerule millager:raid_defenses false` to toggle features. |
