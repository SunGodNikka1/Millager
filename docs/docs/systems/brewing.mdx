# Brewing

Millager adds a **two-stage cauldron brewing system** to produce [Liquor](/content/items/liquor) and [Elixir](/content/items/elixir). This system is entirely separate from the vanilla Brewing Stand.

## Overview

```
Crops → [Liquor Cauldron] → Liquor → [Brewing Cauldron] → Elixir
```

### Stage 1: Liquor Cauldron

1. Place a [Liquor Cauldron](/content/blocks/liquor_cauldron) in the world
2. Fill it with **water** (right-click with water bucket, or wait for rain)
3. Add **crops** — any of: wheat, potato, beetroot, wheat seeds
4. The cauldron begins brewing
5. Collect the Liquor with a **glass bottle**

### Stage 2: Brewing Cauldron

1. Place a [Brewing Cauldron](/content/blocks/brewing_cauldron) in the world
2. Fill it with **Liquor** (right-click with a Liquor bottle)
3. Add **rare ingredients** to begin brewing Elixir
4. The block entity tracks brewing progress
5. Collect the Elixir with a glass bottle

## Ingredients

### Liquor Cauldron Inputs

| Ingredient | Result |
|------------|--------|
| Wheat | Liquor |
| Potato | Liquor |
| Beetroot | Liquor |
| Wheat Seeds | Liquor |

All four ingredients must be used to unlock the **"Local Distillery"** advancement.

### Brewing Cauldron Inputs

| Ingredient | Result |
|------------|--------|
| Rare ingredients | Elixir |

## Related Items

- [Liquor](/content/items/liquor) — the intermediate brew
- [Elixir](/content/items/elixir) — the final product

## Related Advancements

- **"Just a Sip"** — brew your first bottle of Liquor
- **"Local Distillery"** — brew with all 4 ingredients
- **"Wondrous Elixir"** — drink an Elixir
