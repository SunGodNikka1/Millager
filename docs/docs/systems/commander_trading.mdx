# Commander Trading

The **Commander** is a new villager profession added by Millager. Commanders use the [Sand Table](/content/blocks/sand_table) as their job site and offer military-themed trades across 5 levels.

## Job Site

The [Sand Table](/content/blocks/sand_table) is the Commander's Point of Interest (POI). Place one in a village and an unemployed villager will take the Commander profession.

<Callout variant="info">
The Sand Table POI is registered as `millager:command_poi` in the `acquirable_job_site` tag, making it discoverable by villagers alongside vanilla job blocks.
</Callout>

| Buy | Sell |
|-----|------|

| Emerald (x) | Outpost Explorer Map (1) |

| Buy | Sell |

## Explorer Maps

Commander-sold explorer maps point to:
- **Outpost Explorer Map** — nearest Pillager Outpost (or `#millager:on_outpost_exploder_maps` tagged structure)
- **Local Explorer Map** — nearby point of interest

## Farmer Liquor Trades

Farmers also gain two Millager-related trades via datapack tags:

- **Level 1**: Buys Liquor for Emeralds
- **Level 3**: Sells Liquor for Emeralds
