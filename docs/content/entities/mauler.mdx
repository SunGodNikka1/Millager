# Mauler

A durable tank Millager unit. Maulers **repair their own armor** during combat and show visible damage through a crackiness texture layer.

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Health | 32 HP |
| Role | Tank |

## Behavior

- Engages enemies in sustained melee combat
- **Self-repairs armor** when damaged — triggers the **Mauler fixes** sound
- Crackiness visual system — texture changes from pristine to cracked as health decreases
- Absorbs damage for other Millagers by staying at the front
- Part of the **Infantry** spawn pool for [Patrols](/docs/systems/patrols) and [Raid Defense](/docs/systems/raid_defense)

## Crackiness System

The Mauler's texture shows progressive damage:
- **High HP** — clean texture
- **Medium HP** — visible cracks
- **Low HP** — heavily cracked appearance

## Drops

- Amethyst Shards (1–5)
