# Rioter

A heavy infantry Millager unit. Rioters carry a distinctive **Riot Shield** back layer, use a **taunt ability** to draw enemy aggro, and throw projectiles.

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Health | 40 HP |
| Role | Tank / Controller |

## Behavior

- Draws enemy aggro with the **Taunt** ability — forces hostile mobs to target the Rioter
- Throws **Molotov Cocktails** and other projectiles at enemies
- Carries a shield on the back for additional visual presence
- Taunts play the **Rioter taunts** sound
- Part of the **Infantry** spawn pool for [Patrols](/docs/systems/patrols) and [Raid Defense](/docs/systems/raid_defense)

## Taunt

- Forces nearby hostile mobs to re-target the Rioter
- Protects weaker Millagers (Archers, Doctors) from being focused
- Has a cooldown between uses

## Drops

- TNT on a Stick (1–3)
- Molotov Cocktail (1–3, 20% chance)
- Villager Banner Pattern (5% chance)
