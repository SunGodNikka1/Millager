# Doctor

A support Millager unit. Doctors don't fight directly — instead they **construct Iron Golems** and **summon Bee Golems** to defend the village.

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Health | 22 HP |
| Role | Support / Builder |

## Behavior

- Builds **Iron Golems** by placing Fake Iron Blocks and Carved Pumpkins in the classic golem pattern
- Summons **Bee Golems** using Golem Amber from its inventory
- Collects useful items from the ground (Golem Amber, Liquor, TNT)
- Flees from danger using a fly-escape goal when threatened
- Drinks potions when injured
- Part of the **Infantry** spawn pool for [Patrols](/docs/systems/patrols) and [Raid Defense](/docs/systems/raid_defense)

## Equipment

- Carries Fake Iron Blocks and Carved Pumpkins in inventory
- May carry Golem Amber for Bee Golem summoning

## Drops

- Golem Amber (1–3)
- Iron Block (5% chance)
- Carved Pumpkin (5% chance)
