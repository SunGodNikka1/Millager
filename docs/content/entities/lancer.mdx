# Lancer

A mounted cavalry Millager unit. Rides a horse into battle and casts a **Healing Aura** spell that restores health to nearby allies.

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Health | 28 HP |
| Role | Cavalry Support |
| Mount | Horse |
| Special | Healing Aura |

## Behavior

- Rides a horse (spawns with one automatically)
- Casts the **Healing Aura** spell — a ring of healing particles that restores HP to friendly entities in range
- The aura spell plays the **Lancer casts spell** sound
- Aggressive against hostile mobs
- Part of the **Cavalry** spawn pool for [Patrols](/docs/systems/patrols) and [Raid Defense](/docs/systems/raid_defense)

## Healing Aura

The Lancer's signature ability creates a ring of healing particles around its position. The spell:
- Heals nearby villagers and Millagers over time
- Has a visual particle ring effect
- Triggers on a cooldown

## Drops

- Emeralds (1–3)
- Chainmail Chestplate (5% chance)
