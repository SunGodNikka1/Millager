# Swordmaster

An elite melee Millager unit. Swordmasters use a **shield stance** to block incoming attacks and deliver powerful counter-strikes.

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Health | 30 HP |
| Role | Elite Melee |

## Behavior

- Engages enemies in melee combat
- Activates **Shield Stance** — raises shield to block attacks
- The shield block triggers the **Swordmaster blocks** sound
- Delivers counter-attacks after successful blocks
- Has a respawn mechanic — can return to battle after being downed
- Part of the **Infantry** spawn pool for [Patrols](/systems/patrols) and [Raid Defense](/systems/raid_defense)

## Shield Stance

- Blocks incoming melee and projectile attacks
- Limited duration — must recharge between uses
- Visual shield overlay when active

## Drops

- Emeralds (1–3)
- Totem of Undying (2% chance)
