# Scouter

A mounted cavalry scout unit. Scouters ride horses and **blow a reinforcement horn** to summon additional Millagers to the area.

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Health | 26 HP |
| Role | Cavalry Scout |
| Mount | Horse |
| Special | Reinforcement Horn |

## Behavior

- Rides a horse (spawns with one automatically)
- Blows the **Reinforcement Horn** — summons nearby Millager allies to its position
- The horn plays the **Reinforcement horn blares** sound
- Scouts ahead of the main group to locate threats
- Part of the **Cavalry** spawn pool for [Patrols](/docs/systems/patrols) and [Raid Defense](/docs/systems/raid_defense)

## Reinforcement Horn

- Summons friendly Millagers within range to the Scouter's location
- Critical for coordinating Millager forces during [Raid Defense](/docs/systems/raid_defense)

## Drops

- Gunpowder (1–5)
- Custom Firework Rocket (5% chance)
