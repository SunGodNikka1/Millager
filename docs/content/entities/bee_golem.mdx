# Bee Golem

A small glass-and-wax construct. Bee Golems function as **self-guided kamikaze drones** — they fly toward enemies and explode on contact.

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Size | 0.55 × 0.45 |
| Role | Kamikaze Drone |

## Behavior

- Flies toward hostile mobs
- Explodes on contact or when destroyed
- Can be **summoned** by:
  - Using a [Golem Amber](/content/items/golem_amber)
  - A [Doctor](/content/entities/doctor) deploying one in combat
- Plays distinct sounds for each damage stage:
  - **Glass Break** — outer glass shatters
  - **Wax Break** — wax layer cracks
  - **Burst Out** — final explosion
  - **Hurt** — damaged but still functional

## Damage States

The Bee Golem visually degrades through multiple damage states before exploding. Each state has a unique sound cue.

<Callout variant="info">
Bee Golems are temporary — they cannot be repaired or healed. Once summoned, they will eventually shatter.
</Callout>
