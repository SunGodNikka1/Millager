# Golem Amber

A crystallized fragment containing the essence of a Bee Golem. Right-click to summon a friendly [Bee Golem](/content/entities/bee_golem) that will fight for you.

<Callout variant="info">
Summoned Bee Golems are temporary constructs. They will shatter after taking enough damage.
</Callout>

<PrefabObtaining />

## Usage

- Right-click to summon a Bee Golem at the target location
- Can be obtained from [Commander](/docs/systems/commander_trading) trades (level 5)
- Dropped by [Doctor](/content/entities/doctor) Millagers on death
