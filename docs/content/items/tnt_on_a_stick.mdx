# TNT on a Stick

A throwable explosive. Hold right-click to charge, release to throw. The projectile explodes on impact, dealing heavy area damage to hostile mobs.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Usage

- **Hold right-click** to charge the throw (up to full power)
- **Release** to launch the TNT projectile
- Explodes on contact, dealing damage in a radius
- Does **not** destroy blocks
- 1.0 second cooldown between throws
