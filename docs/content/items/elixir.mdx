# Elixir

A wondrous potion that grants **8 positive effects** for 30 minutes each (Luck for 60 minutes), and cures all harmful status effects on consumption. Also capable of **curing NITWIT villagers** into normal villagers.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Effects

| Effect | Duration | Amplifier |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| Strength | 30:00 | I |
| Saturation | 30:00 | I |
| Jump Boost | 30:00 | I |
| Speed | 30:00 | I |
| Haste | 30:00 | I |
| Water Breathing | 30:00 | I |
| Night Vision | 30:00 | I |
| Luck | 60:00 | V |

## Special Interactions

- **Cures harmful effects** on consumption (Poison, Wither, etc.)
- **Cures NITWIT villagers** — right-click a NITWIT with Elixir to transform them into an unemployed villager. See [Elixir & NITWIT Curing](/docs/systems/elixir_interaction).

<Callout variant="info">
The NITWIT-curing interaction unlocks the hidden advancement **"A Cure for the Incurable"**.
</Callout>
