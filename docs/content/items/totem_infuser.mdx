# Totem Infuser

A workstation block used to infuse Totems of Undying with protective enchantments. Right-click to open the infusion GUI.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Usage

- Place in the world and right-click to open the **Totem Infuser** GUI
- Used to transfer enchantments onto Totems of Undying
- See the [Totem Infuser block page](/content/blocks/totem_infuser) for full functionality

<Callout variant="info">
The Totem Infuser requires a Totem of Undying as its base ingredient.
</Callout>
