# Villager Head

A decorative skull block. Can be worn as a helmet or placed on the ground or wall.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Properties

- Can be **worn** in the helmet slot (cosmetic, no armor value)
- Can be **placed** on the ground or mounted on a wall
- Plays the **Villager** instrument when powered by a Note Block
- Dropped when a **Charged Creeper** kills a Villager
- Used as the icon for the Millager creative tab
