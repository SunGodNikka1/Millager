# Totem of Healing

A passive healing charm. While in your inventory, it slowly regenerates your health over time. Exists in three tiers that degrade with use.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Tiers

| Tier | Healing Rate | Durability |
|------|-------------|------------|
| **Totem of Healing** | Fastest | Degrades to Cracked |
| **Cracked Totem of Healing** | Moderate | Degrades to Damaged |
| **Damaged Totem of Healing** | Slowest | Destroys on break |

## Mechanics

- Passively heals while in your inventory (no need to hold)
- Each heal tick has a small chance to degrade the totem
- Degradation path: Totem → Cracked → Damaged → Destroyed
- **Multiple totems stack** — carrying several totems increases healing speed
- Obtained from [Commander](/systems/commander_trading) trades (level 5) and [Rioter](/content/entities/rioter) drops

<Callout variant="info">
The **Multi-Core Healing** advancement requires carrying 2+ Totems of Healing in your inventory.
</Callout>
