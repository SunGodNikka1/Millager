# Molotov Cocktail

A throwable incendiary weapon. On impact, it spawns [Enduring Fire](/content/blocks/timed_fire) — a special flame that never extinguishes naturally.

<PrefabObtaining />

## Usage

- Throw to create a patch of Enduring Fire on impact
- Fire persists indefinitely until manually extinguished
- Used by [Rioter](/content/entities/rioter) Millagers as their primary ranged attack
- 1.0 second cooldown between throws

<Callout variant="danger">
Enduring Fire cannot be extinguished by rain or time. Use water or punch it out manually.
</Callout>
