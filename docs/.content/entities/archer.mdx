# Archer

A ranged combat Millager unit. Armed with a crossbow and specialized arrows, Archers provide fire support from behind the front line.

| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Health | 24 HP |
| Armor | 7 |
| Weapon | Crossbow |
| Role | Ranged DPS |

## Behavior

- Attacks from range with a crossbow
- Crafts **Explosive Arrows** in the field when ammunition runs low
- Picks up tipped arrows from the ground for bonus effects
- Uses predictive aiming with gravity compensation
- Drinks potions when injured
- Part of the **Infantry** spawn pool for [Patrols](/systems/patrols) and [Raid Defense](/systems/raid_defense)

## Drops

- Arrows (1–5)
- Leather Chestplate (5% chance)
