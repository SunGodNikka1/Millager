# Enduring Fire

A special fire block created by [Molotov Cocktails](/content/items/molotov_cocktail) and [Molotov Cocktail +](/content/items/molotov_cocktail_plus). Burns indefinitely until manually extinguished.

## Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Light Level | 15 |
| Collision | No |
| Push Reaction | Destroy |

## Behavior

- Created on impact when a Molotov Cocktail is thrown
- **Cannot be extinguished by rain or time** — unlike vanilla fire
- Must be manually punched out or extinguished with water
- Light level 15 (same as lava and fire)
- The Molotov Cocktail + variant creates a larger spread

<Callout variant="danger">
Enduring Fire does not go out on its own. Clean it up or it will stay forever.
</Callout>
